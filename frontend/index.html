Platform Features

Everything You Need to Save Lives

A complete ecosystem connecting donors, patients, hospitals, and government — all in one place.

🚨

Emergency Mode

One-click emergency requests instantly notify all nearby compatible donors and hospitals via real-time alerts.

🧠

AI Donor Matching

Smart algorithm ranks donors by blood compatibility, distance, response history, and donation recency.

🗺️

Live Map View

Real-time map showing verified donors, hospitals, and open blood requests within your selected radius.

🏥

Hospital Verification

Only certified, government-approved hospitals can verify donors, ensuring maximum safety and trust.

✅

Eligibility Checks

Automatic checks for donation gap (56 days), age, weight, and health status block ineligible donors.

🛡️

Government Oversight

Government admin panel approves hospitals, schedules inspections, and monitors system integrity.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Real-time Socket.IO alerts notify donors when someone nearby needs blood and when they become eligible.

⭐

Donor Reputation

Patients rate donors after successful donations, building a trusted reputation system.

🔐

Privacy Control

Donors choose public, semi-private, or private profiles. Full control over who can see your information.
